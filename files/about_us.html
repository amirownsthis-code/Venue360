About Venue360

What Venue360 Is

Venue360 is a platform made to organize and streamline university events in a easy way. It gives students one place to explore everything happening on campus.

Why It Was Created

Venue360 was created to solve the issue of scattered event details and missing information. Students often miss events because the information is not easily accessible, so Venue360 brings it all into a single system.

What It Helps Students Do

Venue360 helps students discover upcoming events, and stay updated with university announcements. It makes participation simple and improves communication across campus.
